# Code of Conduct

The SevynOS community is committed to a welcoming, technically rigorous, and
harassment-free environment for everyone.

Be respectful. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Assume good faith while
remaining clear about safety and correctness. Do not harass, threaten,
discriminate against, or expose private information about another participant.
Avoid sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, personal attacks, and unwanted
contact.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the
community. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately, and
protect the people affected.

Report conduct concerns privately to `kevonporter@outlook.com`. Do not use a
public issue for sensitive reports. The project will limit disclosure to the
people needed to review and respond to the report.
